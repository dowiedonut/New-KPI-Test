Definitions

Billable utilizationBillable hours per month / expected billable hours per month. 100% = on target. For a silo, it is the whole team combined.
Billable hoursAll hours logged that are not Internal Admin.
Open billable hoursExpected billable hours per month minus billable hours logged. The time a person has available for more client work each month.
Admin hoursHours logged to Internal Admin. Shown for reference.
Expected billingWhat the team can work, billed at the billable hourly rates: expected billable hours per month x billable hourly rate. S+S uses its people from the SOAN India Costing sheet (Shipra, Mayank, Ashmita, and Parita at 50%) at Clerk targets; their hours are not tracked, so S+S has no utilization.
Expected hoursExpected billable hours per month x % allocated to the silo.
Profit margin on actual billing(Actual billing minus cost) / actual billing.
Other overheadPayroll for people not assigned to a silo, shown as one number. Counted in company cost and company profit margin.
Share of capacity billedClient list billing per month / expected billing per month at capacity.
Minimum billable rateThe lowest average billing per billable hour that still leaves a 30% profit after all costs (target margin is a placeholder). Total cost per month / 0.70 / expected billable hours per month.
